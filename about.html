Our Story

A Decade of Tech Transformation

VisionTechie was founded in 2014 by a group of passionate IT professionals and educators who saw a massive gap between what colleges taught and what companies actually needed. Starting with just 30 students and 2 trainers in a small office in Hyderabad, we have grown into one of India's most respected IT training institutes.

Today, with a sprawling campus, 15+ expert trainers, 200+ hiring partners, and 5000+ successful alumni placed at companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, and Microsoft — we are just getting started.
